A LITTLE CLARITY BEFORE YOU GO

Good questions.
Simple answers.

More exploring. Less second-guessing.

How does a booking request work?

Choose a tour, date, and number of guests. Your request appears in My bookings. For a scheduled departure, choose Hold seats to reserve available capacity temporarily for up to 15 minutes. A request alone does not reserve seats.

Can I pay for a trip here?

Planit is currently a portfolio preview, not a live travel operator. Real payments are disabled. Stripe test checkout is available only when configured, and its hosted sandbox verification is still pending. Sample tours are marked Demo.

Where do my saved experiences go?

While logged out, your favorites stay in this browser. Log in to save experiences to your account and find your account favorites on another device.
